You type google.com. Nothing near you knows its number.

Your phone asks the resolver. The resolver's cache is empty, so it asks the root. The root does not know the number either. It only knows who runs .com.
.com only knows who runs google. 

Google's own server knows: 142.250.72.14.

The answer comes back and the resolver writes it down. So, the next time anyone asks, from any tab, it is one hop and instant.

A name is a question. DNS is the chain that answers it, once.
